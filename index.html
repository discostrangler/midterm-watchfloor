              How to read this and what is left out
              • Race and chamber-control odds are Kalshi prices for "Democrat wins": the quoted midpoint of the Democratic contract, hourly from Oct 4 and per minute on election night (Nov 3 5 PM – Nov 4 11 AM ET). The market card shows last trades instead (see below). "Trading closed" and "settled" are Kalshi's own times: Kalshi stops trading, then settles the market. This page has no media-call or AP times, so it does not say how either relates to a race call.
              • Bets are Kalshi trades, which are anonymous. One bet is one taker order: all fills of the same contract and side at the same instant. Its size is what the taker paid. A bet "backs D" when it buys YES on the Democrat or NO on the Republican. The midterm markets include Kalshi's House + Senate balance-of-power market (Democrats sweep, Republicans sweep, or a split). There, only buying YES on a sweep counts as backing a party; YES on a split, and NO on any outcome, count as other. The "Big · midterms" and "Big · all Kalshi" tapes, the big-bet circles in the timeline and the key moments leave out orders priced above 97¢ or below 3¢ (collecting near-certain payouts); the "This market" tape, the flow lanes and the totals include them.
              • Reddit counts come from the Arctic Shift archive of Reddit: national political subreddits, plus one main subreddit per state (r/texas, r/Pennsylvania …), so the map's "Social" layer shows where people were talking. Counts only, no post text.
              • Bluesky is a sample: the top posts for each candidate's name and "<state> election" from Nov 3 11 AM to Nov 4 3 PM ET. It shows attention among popular posts, not total volume. X (Twitter) is not included because its read API is paid.
              • Key moments before election night are the sharpest 24-hour moves in chamber control and in Senate and Governor odds (moves that held for at least 12 hours), the largest bet in each of the six biggest-betting races, hours when the national political subreddits had at least 1.8× their usual comments for that hour, and days the S&P 500 moved 1.5% or more. After election night: the most-traded races Kalshi settled later.
              • Key-moment posts are shown with Bluesky's, Reddit's and X's own embeds, only after you click: Bluesky posts from accounts with 25,000+ followers, Reddit posts with 50+ points from the national subreddits and the state's own subreddit, and X posts that were shared in such Reddit posts. Nothing from those posts is stored on this site; deleted posts stop showing.
              • The House view colors whole states by settled seats, because district boundaries are not drawn. Several states redrew lines for 2026.
              • Gas is AAA's daily regular-gas average as reported by Kalshi's settled AAA markets. Rain is the simple average of NOAA GHCN-Daily stations in each state, not weighted by population.
              • Turnout markets: at 6 PM ET each day, the vote total Kalshi's ladder puts at 50/50. They are thin (median spread about 10¢), and the comparison is the most recent midterm Senate race in the state, so read direction, not level.
              • Streams: eight election-night streams, four left- and four right-leaning. Chat counts come from the four YouTube streams that have a chat replay. Polymarket counts cover comments on its Senate control, House control and balance-of-power events.
              • All of Kalshi: every trade on the exchange from Nov 3 5 PM to Nov 4 11 AM ET, by category from Kalshi's series catalog ("Parlays" are Kalshi's combo markets; "Stocks" are its S&P 500, Nasdaq and other index markets). Other hours are an estimate: markets that opened and closed within a week (games, crypto, daily weather and economics) with their settled volume spread evenly over their open hours, plus the midterm trades; longer-running markets are left out there. Volume is in contracts, which is what Kalshi shows as dollar volume.
              • Two prices appear on this page and can differ in a thin market. Quoted mid is the midpoint of the best bid and ask (map, reading strip, state panel, timeline, heatmap and the election-night panel; from the Democratic contract of the party event, with no last-trade fallback, so a period with no two-sided quote shows as a gap). Last trade is the price of the most recent trade (the market card). The election-night panel shows both, from the Democratic contract only.
              • The market card follows Kalshi's layout: each outcome's percentage is its last traded YES price in the last complete minute before the cursor on election night (the last complete 10 minutes otherwise), so it never includes a trade made after the cursor; Yes and No show the last trade's prices, not the order book. "This market" lists the latest 120 bets in the chosen market up to the cursor, from its last three trading days at most.
              • Beyond the midterms, the market picker has the most-traded Kalshi events in each category: sports games, crypto, stock indexes, other elections, finance, weather and more. They're the events with the most money bet on election night, plus the most-traded events that closed between Oct 4 and Nov 12. Their charts are hourly Kalshi prices (bid/ask midpoint, else the last trade) for up to six markets each, shown once the hour has ended. On election night the charts are per minute and include every bet. Clicking a bet in "Big · all Kalshi" opens its market when it's one of these.
              • Polls: 1,146 general-election polls since Aug 1 for 148 races, from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0) and VoteHub (CC BY 4.0), collected and matched to Kalshi races by a teammate. The market card lists each race's polls up to the cursor and the average D–R margin of the latest five. A pollster marked (D) or (R) was working for that party. Many House races have no public poll.
              • Stocks: daily closes of the S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite and VIX from FRED. U.S. stock markets are closed overnight, so on election night the stocks lane shows a Kalshi-implied S&P 500 (30-minute candles: green up, red down) and Nasdaq-100 (dashed line) instead. Every trade in Kalshi's "S&P / Nasdaq-100 at or above K at 10 AM on Nov 4" markets gives the chance the index ends above each strike. The lane plots the level where that chance crosses 50%, every 5 minutes, as a % change from the Nov 3 close. Kalshi settled those markets at the actual 10 AM values (S&P +1.7%).
              • Every race · 120 days: one row per Senate, Governor and House race (all 435 House seats plus DC's delegate; Kentucky's Senate race had no Kalshi market on the winner). Each cell is the quoted midpoint of the Democratic contract's best bid and ask at that moment (party event; per minute from Oct 20, hourly before), 1 or 0 after Kalshi settles. Brown cells are gaps: the market was open but had no two-sided quote in that period.
              • Not here yet: official vote counts and AP call times. X posts appear only when they were shared in Reddit posts.

              Sources: Kalshi Trade API, AAA via Kalshi, NOAA GHCN-Daily, MEDSL, Arctic Shift (Reddit), Bluesky, Polymarket, YouTube chat replays (counts). Not affiliated with any of them.
